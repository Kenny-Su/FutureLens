# WaitLess

**Delegate the waiting.**

A mountain emerges from the clouds. Fog lifts from a village. The first light
reaches a summit. You know the photograph you want; you just don't know when it
will happen.

WaitLess is an AI photography assistant that watches for the moment you describe.
It checks previews from your camera and saves a photograph when it recognizes
the condition you've agreed on. You choose the composition, then hand over the
waiting.

> “What moment should I wait for?”

The goal is simple: spend less time watching for a change and less time sorting
through photographs taken while nothing was happening.

1. **Talk through your moment.** Describe what you want to happen. WaitLess
   explains when it would take a photo and can highlight objects in the preview
   to show what it means. Correct a misunderstanding, answer a question, or ask
   “what if?” Keep the conversation going until you're ready to approve.
2. **Choose your timing.** Decide when watching begins and how often to check.
   Start now, or set something like “in 20 minutes, check every 10 seconds.”
   Your chosen interval stays fixed throughout the session.
3. **Let WaitLess watch.** Once you approve, it checks the scene on your schedule
   and saves photos when the agreed condition is met. Let the qualifying moments
   collect for you to review, then choose your favorites. Stop whenever you're done.

Use your iPhone through Continuity Camera on your Mac. WaitLess watches previews
and requests high-quality still photos with Apple's photo processing when your
moment arrives. Keep your iPhone connected and your Mac awake while it watches.

Some moments you might delegate:

- “Take photos when the mountain summit is clearly visible through the clouds.”
- “Photograph the village when the fog has lifted enough to reveal it.”
- “Capture the mountain when warm sunrise light reaches its summit.”
- “Take a photo when someone sits on the empty bench.”

You can also explore the experience with a video. Import a clip, discuss the
moment you want, and approve the plan. The video advances at normal speed while
WaitLess watches; each triggered photograph is saved as a frame from the video.

WaitLess is an early prototype for macOS, designed for scenes
that unfold over time. The ambition is to give photographers more freedom during
the wait while keeping the creative decisions in their hands.
